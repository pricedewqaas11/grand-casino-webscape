Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to our most commonly asked questions about our resorts and services.

VIP membership is available by invitation or application. We consider factors such as gaming history, referrals from existing members, and financial verification. All applications are reviewed by our membership committee. Benefits include exclusive access to private gaming areas, personalized service, priority reservations, and special event invitations.

Yes, we maintain an elegant dress code to ensure the sophisticated atmosphere of our resorts. During the day, smart casual attire is acceptable. After 7 PM, more formal attire is required: collared shirts, dress pants and closed shoes for gentlemen; dresses, skirts or dressy pants for ladies. VIP rooms and fine dining restaurants maintain a strict formal dress code at all times.

We accept all major credit and debit cards, wire transfers, and cash. VIP members can establish credit lines with proper verification. For high-value transactions, we also accept cryptocurrency at select locations (Bitcoin, Ethereum). All transactions are secure and confidential, and currency exchange services are available on-site.

Private gaming salons can be reserved by VIP members through our dedicated concierge service. Depending on the exclusivity level, booking may require 24-48 hours advance notice. Standard private rooms are available for a minimum buy-in, while our elite private salons are reserved for established members with specific gaming histories. Contact your personal host or the VIP services desk for assistance.

While our resorts are primarily designed for adult guests, we do welcome families. Children under 18 are not permitted in gaming areas but can enjoy our family-friendly amenities including pools, selected restaurants, and entertainment shows designated as family-appropriate. Some of our properties offer supervised kids' clubs and activities. Age restrictions vary by location, and we recommend checking specific resort policies when booking.
